Six fraternities. One council.

The Independent Interfraternity Council brings together six fraternities at the University of Nevada, Reno, and sets the standards they share.

Join recruitment Meet the chapters

    What we stand for

    Every chapter in the council commits to the four values on our seal. They guide how we recruit, how we host events, and how we hold each other accountable.

    Safety
    Members, guests, and new members look out for one another at every event.
    Integrity
    Chapters keep their word, follow university policy, and own their mistakes.
    Service
    We give time and money to Reno and to the causes our chapters support.
    Unity
    Six different chapters work together as one community on campus.

    Thinking about joining a fraternity?

    Recruitment is your chance to meet all six chapters, see what each one is like, and find the one that fits. There's no cost to sign up and no commitment until you accept a bid.
